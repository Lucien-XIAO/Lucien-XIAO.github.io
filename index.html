About Me

Probability · Statistical Mechanics · Random Interfaces

Incoming PhD researcher at Université Gustave Eiffel · October 2026

I am currently completing my M2 internship at the LAMA (Université Paris-Est Créteil), under the supervision of Arnaud LE NY. My internship focuses on the long-range Ising model and related questions in probability and statistical mechanics. I am also a second year Master's (M2) student in Probability and Random Models (M2-PMA), in the Stochastic Processes track, at Sorbonne Université.

I have been fortunate to work on several research projects, including the study of bi-Lipschitz equivalence of separated nets in Banach spaces with Alexandros ESKENAZIS, and collective motion modeling with Diane PEURICHARD.
