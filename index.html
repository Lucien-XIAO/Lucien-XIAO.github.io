About Me

Beginning in October 2026, I will be a PhD student in mathematics at the LAMA, Université Gustave Eiffel. My doctoral project, Interface Models in Mathematical Statistical Mechanics, will be supervised by Arnaud Le Ny (LAMA, UPEC) and Pierre Monmarché (LAMA, UGE). The project lies at the intersection of probability theory, statistical mechanics, and ergodic theory. Previously, I was a student in the M2 Probabilités et Modèles Aléatoires program at Sorbonne Université.

I have been fortunate to take part in several research projects, including the long-range Ising model with Arnaud Le Ny, bi-Lipschitz equivalence of separated nets in Banach spaces with Alexandros Eskenazis, and collective motion modeling with Diane Peurichard.
